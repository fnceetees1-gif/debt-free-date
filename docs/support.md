# Debt Free Date — Support

Email **thomiandesigns@aol.com** and I'll get back to you.

## Common questions

### I bought Pro but it's showing as the free version

Open **Settings → Restore purchase**. Make sure you're signed in with the same
Apple ID you used to buy it. Restoring is free and can be done as many times as
you need, on any device you own.

### My debt-free date says the payments never clear the debt

That warning appears when a minimum payment is smaller than the interest that
debt accrues each month, so the balance grows instead of shrinking. Check the
APR and minimum payment you entered for each debt, and try adding an extra
monthly payment on the Strategy tab.

### Why did my payoff date change when I switched strategies?

Snowball pays the smallest balance first, which clears individual debts sooner.
Avalanche pays the highest interest rate first, which costs less overall.
Avalanche is never slower or more expensive in total, but Snowball clears your
first debt earlier — which some people find easier to stick with.

### Is my data backed up?

No. Everything stays on your device. If you delete the app or lose the device,
the data is gone — there is no copy on a server. Your Pro purchase is tied to
your Apple ID and can always be restored.

### Does this connect to my bank?

No. The app has no access to any financial account. Every number in it is one
you entered by hand.

## A note on what this app is

Debt Free Date is a calculator. Its projections assume you make exactly the
payments you enter, and they do not account for fees, interest rate changes,
promotional periods ending, or missed payments. It is not financial advice. For
guidance on your specific situation, talk to a qualified financial professional
or a non-profit credit counsellor.
