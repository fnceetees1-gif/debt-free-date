# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 August 2026**

Debt Free Date does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — debt names, balances, interest rates, minimum payments,
and your strategy settings — is stored **only on your device**, using the
operating system's standard app storage. None of it is sent anywhere. There is
no account to create, no server to sync with, and no backup we can read.

If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. We cannot recover it for
you, because we never had a copy.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
- We do not use advertising identifiers, and the app contains no ads.
- We do not use third-party tracking or attribution SDKs.
- We do not have access to your financial accounts. The app never connects to a
  bank, card issuer, or lender. Every figure in the app is one you typed in
  yourself.

## Purchases

Debt Free Date offers a one-time purchase to unlock additional features. That
transaction is handled by Apple, and by RevenueCat, which we use to verify
whether a purchase is valid and to restore it if you reinstall the app or
change devices.

For this to work, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated
identifier for your installation, along with the purchase receipt Apple issues.
It does not receive your name, email address, or any of the debt information you
enter in the app. Apple's own handling of your payment details is governed by
Apple's privacy policy; we never see your payment information.

- RevenueCat's privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>
- Apple's privacy policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

## Notifications

If you turn on payment reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your
device. These are generated on the device itself and are not sent through any
push server.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information
from anyone.
