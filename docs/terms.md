## What Debt Free Date is

Debt Free Date is a **calculator**. You enter your own balances, interest rates
and minimum payments, and it projects when those debts would be paid off.

Its projections assume you make exactly the payments you enter, every month, on
time. They do **not** account for fees, interest rate changes, promotional rates
ending, late charges, missed payments, or anything your lender may do. Real
lenders compound interest in ways that differ between products and
jurisdictions; the app uses a standard monthly approximation.
